# Against Entropy Feedback Log

Append-only log of Quentin's editorial feedback, captured during drafting
sessions. This is the raw signal for the learning loop. Lessons graduate
into `skills/kasseh-writing-guide/SKILL.md` via the promotion rule in the
`against-entropy` skill.

Entry format:

```
## L### | YYYY-MM-DD | post: [slug or general] | phase: [thesis/structure/draft/edit/meta]
Status: raw | recurring | directive | promoted (YYYY-MM-DD) | retired
Feedback: what Quentin said or changed
Before/After: (when a concrete edit exists)
Rule: the generalized instruction for future drafts
```

Statuses: `raw` = seen once. `recurring` = 2+ occurrences, inject into all
draft/editor delegations. `directive` = explicit always/never from Quentin,
inject immediately. `promoted` = encoded in the writing guide, kept here for
history. `retired` = superseded by a later lesson.

---

## L001 | 2026-08-04 | post: general | phase: voice direction
Status: promoted (2026-08-04)
Feedback: Always write from Quentin's perspective, first person, speaking
directly to the reader. Example construction he gave: "let me tell you
about". The old guide's preference for "authoritative, impersonal framing"
is wrong and is superseded.
Rule: Default to first-person voice. Quentin is a person telling the reader
something he knows, not an institution publishing analysis.
Promoted into: writing-guide v2 Narrator Rule, draft-agent voice matching,
editor-agent missing-narrator hard failure.

## L002 | 2026-08-04 | post: general | phase: voice direction
Status: promoted (2026-08-04)
Feedback: Target tone includes Alex Hormozi's registers: blunt, courageous,
straight to the point.
Rule: State claims flat. No hedging, no softening, no "it could be argued".
Say the uncomfortable version. Courage over polish.
Promoted into: writing-guide v2 Bluntness Rules, editor-agent hedge
hard failures.

## L003 | 2026-08-04 | post: general | phase: voice direction
Status: promoted (2026-08-04)
Feedback: Target information density similar to Dan Koe's letters
(letters.thedankoe.com). Every sentence advances the argument.
Rule: Cut filler ruthlessly. If a sentence does not add information,
reframe the idea, or set up the next move, delete it. Density is a feature
Quentin explicitly optimizes for.
Promoted into: writing-guide v2 Density Rules, editor-agent density
hard failures.

Status: directive
Feedback: Feedback given while iterating on drafts must be captured for

Rule: Capture every piece of draft feedback here immediately, during the
